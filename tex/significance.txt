Kirigami parachutes are a promising new class of porous falling bodies but the governing fluid force mechanisms and dynamic stability mechanism of the deployed structure have not yet been identified. This work closes that gap using a novel Biot-Savart far-field boundary condition that enables effectively unbounded free-fall simulations at negligible cost. Applying this to a parametric kirigami geometry, we identify the side-slip flutter mechanism governing stability and derive a simple analytical design rule for stable deployment, substantially advancing understanding of this fascinating new application.
